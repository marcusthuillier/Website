How it works

  1. Fill 10 positions. Prop, Hooker, Lock, Flanker, Number 8, Scrum-half, Fly-half, Centre, Wing, Fullback.
  2. Spin. Hit the spin button and the game randomly lands on a real nation's squad from a real World Cup (New Zealand 1987, South Africa 1995, England 2003...). Not happy with it? Spin again, no limit.
  3. Pick a player. Choose anyone from that squad who still has an open slot in your XV. Spin again for the next one. Repeat until all 10 are filled, mixing nations and decades however it lands.
  4. Build chemistry. Players from the same nation link up, and players who were literally on the same World Cup squad link up even harder.
  5. Pick a mode: a 14-match Test Campaign (plays out in full — final record is what matters, one loss doesn't end it); Rugby World Cup 2027 (drops your XV into a real pool from the actual next tournament's draw, then a real simulated run through the real bracket structure — one loss ends it); or History Mode (pick a real past World Cup, drop into a real pool from it, and play a real run through that year's actual bracket, real opponents at every stage — one loss ends it).
