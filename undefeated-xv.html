How it works
The Draft
Spin for a real nation and year, draft a real player
Hit "Respin all" and the game lands on a real national squad from a real World Cup — New Zealand 1987, South Africa 1995, England 2003. Pick anyone from that squad to fill an open position, then spin again for the next one. Every pick comes from its own fresh spin, so a run can mix a 1987 prop with a 2019 fly-half. Players from the same nation, or who were literally on the same World Cup squad, build chemistry together.
Vanilla JS No Build Step
Three Ways to Play
Test Campaign · Rugby World Cup 2027 · History Mode
Test Campaign plays a full 14-match season against real nations seeded on their real current Elo — no elimination, the final record is what matters. Rugby World Cup 2027 drops your XV into a real pool from the actual next tournament's draw (first-ever 24-team/6-pool format), simulates the other five pools in the background, and routes you through a real bracket using World Rugby's own confirmed Round of 16 pairing rule. History Mode does the same for any past World Cup — your XV replaces one real team in a real pool and plays out a real run through that year's actual bracket, using each opponent's real Elo as it stood right before that specific tournament.

The Ratings
Every rating traces to a real, cited source
A two-stage model: real match-impact ratings (2018–2025) from a companion Player Impact Model for modern players, plus a fully sourced achievement layer for every other era — World Cup wins, World Rugby Player of the Year, Hall of Fame, World Cup captaincy, British & Irish Lions selection, each nation's own all-time records, individual World Cup records, regional titles (Rugby Europe, Pacific Nations Cup, Rugby Africa Cup, South American Championship), and both the 2003 World Cup's officially-published "team of the Tournament" and World Rugby's real annual Dream Team of the Year.
Ranked separately within a production tier and an achievement-only tier, then mapped onto the same bell curve — so a squad drafted entirely from 1987 can compete fairly against one drafted entirely from 2019, on average, rather than one era structurally outranking the other.
